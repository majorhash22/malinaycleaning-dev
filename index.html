Regina's Trusted Professional Cleaning Company Since 2020

At Malinay Cleaning Services Corp., we provide professional residential cleaning, commercial cleaning, office cleaning, carpet cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, and post-construction cleaning across Regina, SK and surrounding areas. Your time is precious — let us handle the cleaning so you can focus on what matters most. Every home and workplace is treated with care, ensuring a spotless, healthy, and comfortable environment you can always count on.

✔ Fully Insured

Rest easy knowing our team is fully insured and background checked, giving you complete peace of mind every time we clean.

✔ Regina's Local Experts

Proudly serving Regina and surrounding communities since 2020, we know what local homes and businesses need.

✔ Satisfaction Guaranteed

We are not happy until you are. If something was missed, we will come back and make it right, no questions asked.

✔ Residential and Commercial
