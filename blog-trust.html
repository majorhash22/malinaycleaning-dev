Trust & Community

Why Regina Homeowners Trust Malinay Cleaning Services

By Malinay Cleaning Team  ·  Regina, Saskatchewan  ·  April 2026

Let's be honest — letting someone into your home is a big deal. It doesn't matter if it's the first time or the fiftieth. Your home is your space, your sanctuary, the place where your family eats, sleeps, laughs, and lives. So when you open your front door to a cleaning service, you're not just hiring someone to mop your floors. You're placing a whole lot of trust in another person.

That's something we at Malinay Cleaning Services never take lightly. Over the years, we've had the privilege of serving families, professionals, seniors, and business owners right here in Regina. And the question we get asked most — especially from people who've never hired a cleaning service before — is: "How do I know I can trust you?"

It's a fair question. A great question, actually. And we want to answer it honestly.

We're From Regina. This Is Our Community Too.

There's a difference between a big national franchise that sends whoever is available and a local team that genuinely knows the city they serve. We're not a call centre somewhere across the country routing jobs to strangers. We are your neighbours. We shop at the same grocery stores. Our kids might go to the same schools.


We understand what Regina winters do to a home — the mud, the salt, the dust that seems to find its way into every corner — and we know exactly what it takes to keep your home feeling clean and fresh through every season. When you call Malinay, you're talking to real people who care about the reputation they've built right here in this city. And in a city like Regina, word travels fast. We take enormous pride in what people say about us.

Consistency You Can Actually Count On

One of the biggest frustrations people have with cleaning services is inconsistency. You get an amazing clean the first time, and then the next visit feels rushed and half-done. We've heard this story from so many of our clients — it's actually one of the main reasons they switched to us.


At Malinay, we follow a thorough cleaning process every single visit. Not just when we're trying to impress a new client — every time. Because we believe you deserve the same quality on your tenth clean as you got on your first.

We Treat Your Home Like It's Our Own

This might sound like something every cleaning company says, but we mean it in a very practical way. We're careful with your belongings. We notice the things that matter to you. We don't move your family photos and forget to put them back. We don't use harsh products on surfaces that need gentle care. We pay attention — because the details are what make a home feel truly clean, not just surface-level tidy.


Our team is trained not just in cleaning techniques but in respect. Respect for your space, your privacy, and your time.

Real People, Not Just a Service

We know that trust is built over time, not just promised in an ad. That's why so many of our clients in Regina have been with us for years. They started with one clean, saw the difference, and never looked back. Some of them refer their friends and family to us — and honestly, that means more to us than any marketing campaign ever could.


We're proud of every home we've had the honour of caring for. And if you've been on the fence about hiring a cleaning service, we'd love the chance to show you — not just tell you — why so many Regina homeowners trust Malinay.

Ready to Experience the Malinay Difference?

Your home deserves to feel its best. Whether you need a one-time deep clean or regular maintenance visits, we're here and ready to help.
