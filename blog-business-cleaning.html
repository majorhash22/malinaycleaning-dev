How Often Should Your Regina Business Be Professionally Cleaned?

By Malinay Cleaning Team  ·  Regina, Saskatchewan  ·  April 2026

Running a business in Regina means wearing a lot of hats. You're managing staff, serving customers, handling finances, and trying to grow — the last thing on your mind is probably your cleaning schedule. But here's the thing: the cleanliness of your business space says something about you before you even say a word to a client or customer.

People notice. They always do. A clean, well-maintained space tells your clients that you're professional, that you care about details, and that you take pride in what you do. A dusty reception desk, grimy washrooms, or sticky floors? That tells a different story — one you definitely don't want to be telling.

So how often should your Regina business actually be professionally cleaned? The honest answer is: it depends. But let's break it down.

It Depends on Your Type of Business

Retail Stores

If you have customers walking through your doors every day, your space needs to look its best — always. High-traffic retail spaces should be professionally cleaned at least 3 to 5 times per week, with daily attention to floors, washrooms, and entry areas. First impressions happen the moment a customer walks in, and in retail, that impression directly affects whether they buy.

Restaurants and Food Service

This one is non-negotiable. Restaurants in Regina are subject to health inspections, and cleanliness isn't just about appearances — it's about safety. Professional deep cleaning should happen daily for kitchens and food prep areas, with thorough front-of-house cleaning multiple times per week. Grease buildup, food residue, and bacteria don't take days off, and neither should your cleaning schedule.

Office Spaces

For a typical office environment, a professional clean 2 to 3 times per week is generally the sweet spot. High-touch surfaces like door handles, light switches, keyboards, and shared equipment need regular attention, especially during cold and flu season on the prairies. If your office has a high volume of client visits, bump that up to daily cleaning for washrooms and common areas.

Medical and Healthcare Facilities

If your business involves patient care or any kind of healthcare, the standard is much higher. Medical facilities should be professionally cleaned and sanitized daily, without exception. Cross-contamination risks are real, and your patients and staff deserve an environment that is genuinely hygienic, not just visually tidy.

Warehouses and Industrial Spaces

These spaces often get overlooked because they don't see client foot traffic — but a clean warehouse is a safer warehouse. Dust, debris, and clutter contribute to workplace accidents and equipment issues. Professional cleaning once or twice a week is typically appropriate, with more frequent attention to lunchrooms, washrooms, and office areas within the facility.

Small Professional Offices

For smaller operations — a one or two-person office, a consulting space, or a studio — a professional clean once a week is usually sufficient. It's also one of the best investments you can make in your own productivity. There's something about walking into a clean workspace that puts you in the right mindset to do your best work.

Signs Your Business Needs to Be Cleaned More Often

Not sure if your current schedule is enough? Here are the signs it's time to increase your frequency:

  • Washrooms smell unpleasant before the end of the business day
  • Dust is visibly building up on surfaces between cleans
  • Clients or customers have made comments — even subtle ones
  • Staff are frequently getting sick
  • Your floors look dull or grimy within a day or two of cleaning
  • Your kitchen or break room never quite feels clean

Don't Forget the Seasonal Deep Clean

Regardless of your industry, every Regina business should schedule a thorough seasonal deep clean — ideally in spring and fall. This goes beyond regular maintenance and covers carpets, windows, vents, light fixtures, walls, baseboards, and storage areas. Saskatchewan winters are hard on buildings, and a proper spring deep clean resets your space for the busier seasons ahead.
